How We Service Your Purifier

Our Service Process

Maintaining clean water is an active, structured process. Here is how we support you year-round.

01

Water Quality Check

Our service technicians test input TDS levels, hardness, and chemical impurities to customize purifier filtration rates.

02

Technicians clean the unit and swap sediment, carbon, and RO membrane filters at pre-scheduled intervals to keep water pure.

03
